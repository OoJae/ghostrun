Privacy and AI

GHOSTRUN, a hackathon entry. No account, no login, no ads, no tracking.

Made with AI

Every world, character line, Host answer and edit here is generated by AI models, then checked by a deterministic proof before it is trusted. The worlds are not real places and the characters are not real people. The receipt says what was verified, what was played and what was only modelled.

What you type, say or show

  • Prompts and edits go to our server and on to the AI providers that write the world: Cerebras (open models) and Anthropic (Claude). Their own terms govern what they keep. Don't put personal information in a prompt.
  • Voice is turned into text by your browser's own speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). No audio reaches us; we only receive the text.
  • A photo you attach is sent to Anthropic to describe its setting and colours. We never store it.

What we keep, and why

  • Built worlds for repeated prompts (the prompt text and the world), so the same prompt replays instantly. Marked "cached" whenever you see one.
  • Proof receipts for worlds proved in the cloud, keyed by the world's hash.
  • What you share: a shared run (the world and the inputs of your run), a best time (the name you type, if any), a remix's lineage. Anyone with the link can open it.
  • Reports: "Report" and "This is wrong" keep your note and the run, so it can be replayed and fixed.
  • Abuse limits: your IP address is never stored. A keyed hash of it is kept with spending caps (reset daily), reports and shares, so one visitor can't spend the demo's budget or flood the board.

We keep these while the demo runs (through at least 31 December 2026), then delete them.

Cookies and your browser's storage

  • One cookie, gr_sess: a signed 30-minute session set after Cloudflare Turnstile's check that you're not a bot. It lets the server apply limits fairly; it isn't used to follow you.
  • Your browser's local storage keeps your own settings on your device only: sound muted, the ghost hidden, your language, your name for the board, VR comfort settings, and whether you've seen the voice note.
  • A judge's link carries an access token; it is moved out of the address bar into this tab's session storage and is gone when the tab closes.

Contact

To have something removed (a shared run, a board entry, a report), contact the author through the project's GitHub repository.
